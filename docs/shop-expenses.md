# Shop expenses

The admin can use Expenses and Expense categories.
Staff can use Expenses only when the admin gives them permission.
To give permission, open Employees, edit the staff member, tick Expenses, and save.
Add an expense category before recording an expense.
Open Expense categories, choose New, enter the category name, and save.
Open Expenses, choose New, enter the amount, date, category, and description, then choose Cash as the payment type.
Save the expense to record money paid from the drawer.
To see the month total, open Reports > Summary Reports > Expenses by categories.
The Arabic report name is تقرير المصاريف حسب الفئات.
